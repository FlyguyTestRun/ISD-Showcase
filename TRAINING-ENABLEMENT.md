# Training & Team Enablement Artifacts

This portfolio intentionally includes training-oriented documentation to demonstrate senior-level mentorship and operational handoff capability.

## Enablement Principles Used

1. **Runbook-first delivery:** every major workflow has a documented path to execution.
2. **Reproducible demos:** sample data and scripts allow safe re-runs.
3. **Operational clarity:** steps, prerequisites, and validation checks are explicit.
4. **Knowledge transfer:** documentation supports both engineers and support staff.

## Suggested Team Onboarding Flow (2 Weeks)

### Week 1: Systems Foundations
- Day 1-2: Identity lifecycle walkthrough (`K12-Identity-Management/`)
- Day 3: Backup/DR runbook walkthrough (`Backup-DR-Automation/`)
- Day 4-5: Dell health and lifecycle workflows (`Dell-Hardware-Management/`)

### Week 2: Network + Reporting
- Day 1-2: Segmentation architecture and policy boundaries (`K12-Network-Architecture/`)
- Day 3: KPI dashboard interpretation (`Power-BI-Dashboards/`)
- Day 4-5: Controlled demo execution and handoff

## Mentoring Outcomes This Portfolio Supports

- Faster onboarding for junior engineers
- More consistent change execution
- Better support escalation quality
- Improved leadership visibility through standardized reporting
