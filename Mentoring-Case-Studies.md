# Mentoring Case Studies (Senior Engineer Enablement)

## Case Study 1: Junior Engineer Identity Automation Onboarding

**Situation:** New engineer needed to support student account provisioning during enrollment surge.  
**Approach:** Paired walkthrough of `K12-Identity-Management/`, then guided execution using `sample-students.csv` and batch script workflow.  
**Mentoring Focus:** naming conventions, OU placement logic, audit logging expectations, safe validation before execution.  
**Outcome:** Engineer independently executed batch onboarding checklist and documented exceptions for review.

---

## Case Study 2: Cross-Team Backup/DR Readiness Coaching

**Situation:** Support staff needed better understanding of backup failures vs. true DR risk.  
**Approach:** Used backup runbooks and architecture docs to teach difference between job failure triage, SLA tracking, and full recovery validation.  
**Mentoring Focus:** escalation triggers, evidence capture, and post-incident communication structure.  
**Outcome:** Team improved ticket quality and reduced unnecessary escalations by applying runbook decision points.

---

## Case Study 3: Network Change Confidence Building

**Situation:** Junior staff hesitant to execute network changes due to rollback uncertainty.  
**Approach:** Introduced change-window checklist + RCA template and rehearsed a low-risk change in controlled sequence.  
**Mentoring Focus:** pre-change baseline capture, explicit rollback criteria, and post-change verification discipline.  
**Outcome:** Staff completed supervised change with full evidence trail and clearer operational confidence.

---

## Mentoring Principles Demonstrated

- Teach *why* behind runbook steps, not only *how*
- Use repeatable templates to reduce decision fatigue under pressure
- Build confidence through staged execution and clear rollback paths
- Emphasize documentation quality as a force multiplier for the team
